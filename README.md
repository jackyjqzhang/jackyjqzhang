### Research focus

- **Medical AI** — multimodal clinical decision support, with an emphasis on models whose reasoning can be inspected rather than only their outputs.
- **Long-form video understanding** — modelling hour-scale monitoring footage (bedside cameras, ICU observation, procedure video) where the clinically meaningful signal is spread over time.
- **Healthcare IoT** — fusing device and sensor streams with imaging data so that monitoring is continuous, not sampled.
- **Clinical translation & validation** — external, multi-site and prospective evaluation, robustness under distribution shift, and reporting that distinguishes internal testing from genuinely external testing.
